How to play

Tap the felt to point the cue roughly. Then drag anywhere around the cue ball to turn it gradually; the slower you drag, the finer it turns. A tap on ‹ › moves the cue a twentieth of a degree, and holding starts slow and speeds up. Slide the force bar and tap the small ball to strike it high for follow, low for draw, or to one side. Then press Strike.

Sink your group, solids or stripes, then the eight. After a foul the other player drags the cue ball anywhere and presses Set ball. Reset (↺) puts aim, force, spin, elevation and view back to their starting places.

Cue opens finer controls. Fine aim turns the cue by hundredths of a degree, Force takes exact percentages, and Elevation raises the butt of the cue. Raise the cue and add side spin to curve the cue ball around a blocker.

Best shot tests shots with the real physics. It looks for a direct pot first, then a one or two cushion bank or kick, and never counts anything needing more cushions. Among those it picks the one that leaves the best next shot, and the button tells you which kind it chose. With ball in hand it places the cue ball too. If nothing pots, it lines up a safety.

Assist runs your shot ahead of time with the real physics and draws where the cue ball and the first two balls will go. A gold ring marks a pocket your ball will drop into; a red ring warns of a scratch or the wrong ball.

Against the computer, you are player one. Easy misses often, Medium is steady, Hard rarely misses.

Trails draw a fading streak behind every moving ball so you can follow the break; switch them off in Menu, Display. After each shot, Replay (top left of the table) plays it again; Slow plays it at a third of the speed. Stats keeps this game’s numbers and your all-time record on this device. In Sound and touch you can turn effects, the theme’s ambience and haptic taps on or off.

Keyboard: arrows aim and set force, space strikes, V changes view, A toggles assist, B finds the best shot, C opens the cue controls, T turns trails on or off, R resets the controls.
